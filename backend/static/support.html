Common questions

  • How do I move my progress to a new device? Open Settings → Cloud save, tap Create save code, then enter that code on the new device. Keep the code private.
  • How do I unlock Boss Rush? Beat the three sector bosses (they appear after levels 20, 25 and 30).
  • Someone has an offensive name. Tap the flag next to their name on any leaderboard to report or block them.
  • Can I change my handle? Yes, in Settings. Handles are 3–16 letters, numbers or _.
  • Can you delete my leaderboard entries or cloud save? Yes, email us with your handle (and save code, if you have one).
